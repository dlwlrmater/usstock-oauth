usstock

Personal Google Drive Utility · 个人 Google Drive 文件管理工具

usstock is a private, owner-operated desktop utility for organizing and archiving files in Google Drive accounts explicitly authorized by their owners. It is not a public file-download service.

本工具仅供账号所有者个人使用，用于查询文件信息、读取获许可的文件、上传文件副本及按本人配置管理文件共享权限。仅处理本人拥有或已获得复制、处理许可的文件，不提供公众注册或对外开放文件访问。

Google authorization

Google OAuth is used to access only the accounts explicitly authorized by the owner. The owner can revoke access at any time in Google Account settings. This public website does not request Google sign-in, collect access tokens, or host private documents.

Privacy policy / 隐私说明

Support: contact the repository owner. Please do not include tokens or private files in public issues.
